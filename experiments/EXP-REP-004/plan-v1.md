## Metrics and attribution
1. Body execution integrity: validation, binding witnesses, invoked interpreter, mutation changes predicted transition without modifying prior; first action LQ vs LA differs on predeclared fixture, both paid and legal.
2. Primary utility: 64-state-action exact transition accuracy per committed world/budget; abstention 0 for AULC, in parallel report coverage, error given commitment and cost of abstention.
3. Query ability: paid setup+intervention actions, actual informative responses, optimal-witness opportunity checks as independent diagnostics.
4. Effect attribution: matched-arm LQ−LA, FA, LR, ER, BODY-OFF, STATIC-MACRO signed cluster effects, episode-only controls, binding/role-permutation sensitivity, negative cases and uncertainty.
5. Training scaling: distinct family n, body count and abstraction diversity, transfer outcome; identical pass idempotence and source hashes.
6. Full cost: separate actual action count, simulator/model checks, expanded body primitives, CPU/wall/RSS for WAKE/SLEEP/DREAM/USE/AUDIT; amortize over N=1/32/128/1024 independently of action cost.
