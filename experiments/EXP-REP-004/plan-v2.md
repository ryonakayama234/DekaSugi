## Executed source and sampling

- Boolean n=4, 60 legal atomic edges (direct or one guard bit), <=2 edges and XOR-from-prestate interpretation.
- 1,831 syntactic candidates; 1,735 distinct transition signatures; 85 isomorphism families. Deterministic sorted family-source manifest digest: 440547723d7f7a4ef5f8302c796d8fefc847dfd451be681a3ff7a7d38f08d052.
- TRAIN: 32 distinct inferred semantic families / 64 paid worlds (two differently renamed examples each), with 100 charged operations per world (6,400 train actions).
- Evaluation: 12 seen families/new IDs, 12 unseen CHAIN composition families, 12 other unseen in-grammar families, 12 OOD 3-edge worlds. Source family members are disjoint for distinct heldout groups, but the generating program is shared; not independent source custody.
- ARMS: LQ, LA, LR, ER, FA, FX, BODY-OFF, STATIC-MACRO; budgets 0,1,2,4,8. Trial total 48*8*5=1920; 16 states x4 pulse transitions per committed trial =122880 evaluator-only audits.
- Raw score and checksums are retained regardless of sign. Fixed estimate is mean of 5 budget accuracies within family, then mean across 12 composition-family effects. ABSTAIN utility 0, and selective coverage/errors also reported. No false independence claim for 64 transitions or five budgets.
- Resource: charged train operations, paid use actions, model checks, logical body invocations and CPU-time are recorded separately. Cache reuse and offline preparation are not yet matched as a full lifetime CPU-cost claim.

## Actual engineering gates

1. Enumerate 85 role-invariant families and reject overlap between TRAIN and held-out structural groups; verify no forged costs, forged set or broken trace.
2. Produce learned conditional and concurrent composition AST from paid public histories, with >=3 distinct inferred family supports and negative witnesses where observed.
3. Strictly save/load Snapshot, refuse modified AST/receipt, execute learned AST, demonstrate a first paid question change under identical legal conditioned history, and detect a body-mutation prediction change. Mechanism fixtures are development-only, never scored.
4. Preserve all 1920 signed trial results and per-world action traces, independent post-commit oracle checks and strong fixed controls. An absent formal independent reviewer cannot be described as a completed external scientific review.
5. Report NULL/NEGATIVE/INVALID and all violations. No automatic merge, formal holdout, model promotion or learning claim from technical CI alone.

## Observed outcome and next formal prerequisite

The disclosed engineering trial has negative query value (UNSEEN_COMPOSITION LQ 0.700000 vs LA 0.705208 and FA 0.760417). STATIC-MACRO ties LQ across every group and OOD abstention is zero. Therefore it **fails** the exploratory +0.02 benefit/9-of-12-family criteria. The next research problem is discriminating which abstractions are truly learned rather than human-provided fixed macro grammar, identifying relevant role bindings, and checking safe out-of-grammar uncertainty. No F0 may be opened on these trained/tuned source families.
