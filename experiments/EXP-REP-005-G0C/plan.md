## Hypothesis
The source-only pilot tests exact H0 expressibility, role-orbit aliasing and whether a constructed closed class can have an adaptive inquiry advantage without claiming acquired intelligence.

## Competing hypotheses
- H-R: law can be outside the previous 61-model H0 but inside the declared finite extended source grammar.
- H-S: syntactic diversity and different role bindings need not be distinct semantic orbits.
- H-O: adaptive legal public interventions can have greater closed-class expected MAP value than the strongest nonadaptive schedule, for some constructed classes.
- H-F: the present 12 human-specified production roots do **not** imply 12 statistically independent causal families; source/power must be separately certified.
