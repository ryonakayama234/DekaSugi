## Interpretation and decision
The preregistered experiment supports safe deterministic skill candidate extraction and a limited same-family search improvement under bounded expanded AST cost. The negative reverse task gets worse; increasing library breadth can increase exploration work. Numerical thresholds are satisfied, but a trained macro is **not yet accepted**. An independent code/research review, wider unrelated task families, runtime primitive-call accounting and fresh holdouts must precede broader claims or default promotion.

Future changes to thresholds or algorithms after seeing public holdout data require a new holdout version/EXP identifier.
