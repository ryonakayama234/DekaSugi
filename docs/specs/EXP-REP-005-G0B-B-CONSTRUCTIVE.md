## Research hypothesis and falsifiers
H-CONSTRUCT: composing independently declared causal motifs can yield genuinely distinct *meaning* and role orbits, with explicit generator ancestry, and afford reproducible reachable interventions.
H-QUERY: some produced worlds have a legally reachable public history where a feasible adaptive query outperforms a fixed comparator at the same paid budget; opportunity can be zero or not certified.
H-REP: a candidate can produce a public contradiction to deterministic memoryless predictions while a typed extended predicate predicts the result, **without** presupposing that learning this predicate outperforms preinstalled STATIC-Hplus.
H-NULL: object count, legal actions or semantics are inadequate; the actual attained count/opportunity is low. Report STOP rather than renaming duplicates or inflating quotas.

## Constructive sources — proposed, NOT a frozen grammar
1. Start with isolated source motif constructors: no effect, direct/conditional, lagged, composed/mediated, competing XOR, inhibitory/feedback, subject to separate typed semantics approval. Generator ancestry records the mechanism constructor, its parent DAG and composition operators before object IDs and seeds.
2. Expand by typed composition with explicit maximum objects/edges/lag/guards, fixed source seeds and candidate budget. Preserve G0b-A's 4-object V3.1 as a historical reference, **not** a silently modified population; any n=5/6 extension requires separate numbered grammar version and reviewed resource estimate.
3. Perform cheap structural normalization as a filter, then authoritative complete defined-domain semantics for any candidate claimed distinct, all 24 permutations for n=4 (n! for larger n), and independent reference/oracle checks for representative and adversarial cases. Hashes alone are indexes, not equality proofs; exact signature equality must guard collisions.
4. Maintain two separate equivalences: full typed transition semantics and reachable legal public histories from common start within a declared paid horizon. Do not classify unreachable-only differences as effective active-learning opportunities.
5. Track source parentage across composition, source family roots and role orbit. Split by declared independent root/mechanism family, never object relabeling, arbitrary seeds or correlated instances. Independent causal-family claim remains relative to a preregistered generator taxonomy and external method review.
6. For each **declared closed candidate set C**, run bounded worst-case adaptive identification with public-state/history/budget in memo keys; return ID/UNID only after complete proof, otherwise NOT_CERTIFIED. Separately compute legal feasible-query value versus comparator under identical prior, horizon, paid cost and observable data. Distinguish source-only diagnostics from learner access.
7. Rather than attempt full 100M laws, construct a *finite prospective sublanguage Hplus_C* with complete independently checked closure and capacity-matched fixed control. Claims relative to Hplus_C are allowed **only after exhaustive certification of that sublanguage**. Full Hplus_G/H0_G membership continues NOT_CERTIFIED until an appropriate independent proof exists. Do not relabel Hplus_C as Hplus_G.

### Critical identification pitfall
A semantic novelty witness against H0_all (same public state and action, different observed successors under distinct histories) proves *memorylessness is insufficient*; it does not prove the learned concept beats a static, equally expressive Hplus. Positive action opportunity requires a *feasible* legal decision tree with better verified utility, not an unattained optimal upper bound. A target with no such opportunity is a negative result, not a defective sample.

## Evaluation contract to freeze later, not now
Evidence levels: L0 representability; L1 learnable/public distinguishability; L2 legal inquiry usefulness; L3 new-family transfer and learned-body causal effect; L4 general learning-to-learn. Source construction only measures G0b feasibility, never L3 or L4.

Future controls: equally expressive STATIC-Hplus and STATIC-MACRO, no-learning memoryless, policy-only learned, episodic retrieval, BODY-OFF, random/fixed and strong active; stronger fixed upper bounds are diagnostics. Record paid TRAIN, source construction/certification, training epochs and optimizer steps **separately**; repeated epochs are not additional environmental data. Use matched family splits and action/inference/CPU budgets, fresh and independent prospective evaluation, and family-cluster intervals.

Define the training series prospectively at exposure counts 0, 8, 16, 32, 64 only after a valid family source and sample-size audit. Within each exposure condition, compare fixed learner vs multiple passes over identical traces; record both passes and elapsed compute. Different passes may change the selected predicate; improvement on **fresh** families, beyond capacity-matched controls, is the actual test.

## Non-goals, safety and stop rules
- This document does not change frozen V3.1, historical signed runs, prior PRs, sealed DEV/FINAL, source custody, or any existing scoring entrypoint.
- No arbitrary generated code execution, no hidden law/source fields in the public learner API, no auto library promotion or main merge.
- STOP if semantic enumeration or proof exceeds explicitly recorded node/time/memory/candidate caps; reject aliases, role-equivalent and ancestry-overlap splits. Return reasons and evidence rather than fabricated labels.
- STOP before statistical power claims when independent families are absent. Proposed target of 80 is not a required quota and has not been demonstrated.
- D0 acceptance: method-reviewable proposed diagram, typed data flow, constraints, NOT_RUN ledger and stable Issue/PR links. Subsequent code and scientific evaluation require separate reviews/approval.
