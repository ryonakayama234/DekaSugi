# ADR-REP-003 — Opportunity before model expansion
id: ADR-REP-003
date: 2026-11-01
updated: 2026-11-01
tags: architecture, decision, active-learning
status: PROPOSED / NOT_ACCEPTED
context: EXP-REP-002 corrected LQ=FA in all four strata, public role hub shortcut and negative DISTRACTOR transfer.

Decision proposal: implement a **pre-scoring identifiability and reachability audit** under anonymous roles and conditional two-step actions before expanding learner complexity. Use an optimized fixed active FA and concept-body ablation; do not interpret an LQ−LA advantage alone as concept-specific value. Track diversity and effective evidence, not number of renamed worlds or repeated deterministic replays.

Alternatives: merge-only engineering hardening (necessary dependency), deterministic epoch/replay sweep (limited educational value), SGD/Dream policy (premature), unconstrained cross-domain dialogue (not attributable now). All remain available after this mechanism gate. No changes to public prototype or formal holdout.

Consequences: extra evaluator and cost-ledger work, deliberate risk of scientifically null outcome, but greatly reduced chance of mistaking role labels, underpowered controls or a synthetic favorable setup for acquired intelligence.
