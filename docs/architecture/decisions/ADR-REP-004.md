# ADR-REP-004 — Executable concept before neural/epoch optimization

id: ADR-REP-004
status: PROPOSED / OWNER_REVIEW_PENDING
updated: 2026-11-02

## Context
REP003 revealed acquired class frequency support but no executed Snapshot.library concept and no changed inquiry action across 288 matched cases. Growing training worlds or adding an SGD optimizer cannot validate a missing body→prediction/question path. Full expressive fixed search may legitimately tie a learned-body agent under equal resources.

## Decision
Prioritize a variable-bound typed pure ConceptBody learned solely from paid public experience, explicitly interpreted on restored Snapshot, followed by deterministic counterfactual body mutation, action-choice activation and then a fresh disclosed paired 8-arm engineering pilot. World models and inquiry action/cost rules are shared. Distinct semantic families rather than opaque IDs define independent transfer units.

## Alternatives considered
- **Repeat/epoch sweeps on present REP003:** rejected as the primary fix; no gradient weights and duplicate evidence tests idempotence only.
- **Always-rich fixed grammar/optimized planner:** retained as FA; an unusually strong but fair explanatory rival.
- **PyTorch/neural Wake-Sleep or LLM planner:** deferred until reliable concept-to-action training signal and leakage-safe causal counterfactual comparison exist.
- **Open-ended primitive/metagrammar invention:** higher research value but currently hard to attribute; bounded 2-edge composition first.
- **Immediate baseline/release freeze:** preserve reproducible engineering source only; deny validated Local Learning Model promotion.

## Consequences
One substantial end-to-end engineering run is in scope after gate review, including actual TRAIN, Snapshot use and disclosed scoring. No formal F0/FINAL, historical sealed data access, code merge, automatic promotion or cross-domain intelligence claim. Safety and evidence costs take precedence over apparent AULC gains.
