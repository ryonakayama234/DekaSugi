## Scientific decision state

Pilot restricted fixture has at most fourteen object-renaming structural orbits. Therefore the planned eighty independent families **cannot come from that fixture**. The full generic typed Hplus language and independent generator have not been certified, so do not extrapolate the bound to future extended generators. Independent hypothesis identification across all legal public answer branches is a different question from a memoryless contradiction witness; G0 source methods now test that distinction.

- G0a: bounded method code implemented on #147; synthetic CI records must be read at exact head. This is **not** completion of G0b.
- G0b: STOP for current restricted fixture; future experiment redesign, structural independence and evaluator protocol require independent methodology review.
- G1–G6: no real paid learning, finalized prediction scoring, fresh use advantage or stable release.
